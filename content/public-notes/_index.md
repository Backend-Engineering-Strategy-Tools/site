Notes on DevOps, cloud-native infrastructure, and related tools.

---

Working notes on the tools and practices I actually use not tutorials, not comprehensive guides. 

Raw and opinionated rather than polished.

The idea is straightforward: write things down while they're fresh, publish them rather than leaving them to rot in a private notebook, and let the notes be useful to others who work the same way. 

Accountability helps too it is harder to half-understand something when you have to articulate it.

Read them as a snapshot, not a reference. They are incomplete by design, occasionally wrong about things I have since corrected in practice, and weighted towards whatever I was solving at the time. If something looks off, it probably means I learned better later!
